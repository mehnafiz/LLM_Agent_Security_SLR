# Screening report

## Scope and method

Screening followed `00_PROTOCOL/Eligibility_Criteria.md`. The Master database and IEEE Top 30 remain separate overlapping registers; their counts are not additive. Topic relevance, full-text relevance, PDF identity/version, publication type, indexing, venue quality, and final eligibility are recorded as separate decisions.

For the Master register, 30 DOI-matched abstracts came from the IEEE source collection and 122 additional official abstracts were retrieved from linked source pages and recorded with their URLs in the workbook. Six remaining records have title/snippet evidence only; those snippets are explicitly not treated as abstracts. Retain means proceed to full-text review, uncertain means scope needs adjudication, and exclude is limited to the stated screening stage.

Local PDFs were text-extracted with macOS PDFKit and reviewed for topic relevance. This does not certify venue quality or indexing. Missing documents or missing abstracts are not exclusions.

## Master database: 158 records

- Abstract-based title/abstract screen: 152 records; current outcomes are 123 retain, 18 uncertain, and 11 exclude.
- Title/snippet-only screen, abstract unavailable: 6 records. Their current outcomes remain in the workbook and are not upgraded to abstract-based decisions.
- Full text reviewed: 36 records; 34 relevant at the full-text relevance stage and 2 out of scope. One relevant workshop paper is separately excluded by the protocol publication-type rule. Out-of-scope decisions cite the reviewed PDF evidence.
- Full-text review pending: 122 records, including records excluded earlier in screening.
- The Master exclusion register lists 15 decisions from the current Master database, with stage, rationale, and evidence source.
- Final protocol eligibility remains pending. No record is called eligible until publication/version, indexing, and dated venue-quality gates are evidenced.

## IEEE Top 30 register: 30 records

- All 30 records have title/abstract screening decisions in the register: 26 retained, 3 uncertain pending scope evidence, and 1 excluded as a short paper.
- 12 linked local PDFs were reviewed: 9 relevant, 1 uncertain, and 2 out of scope. The other 18 have no linked local PDF and remain pending at full text.
- No record is finally eligible yet; protocol gates remain to be verified.
