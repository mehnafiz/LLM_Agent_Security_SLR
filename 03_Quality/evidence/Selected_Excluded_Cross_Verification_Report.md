## What was verified

Conference letter ranks come from the ICORE2026 export downloaded from `https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/` on 2026-11-07 (`03_Quality/evidence/ICORE2026_CORE.csv`, 987 rows). A rank was assigned only when the proceedings title contained that entry’s name, or its acronym together with a confirming title word. Workshops, Findings, industry tracks, companion volumes, supplemental volumes, short-paper volumes, posters, and demos were not given the main-track rank.

JCR and Scopus CiteScore were not accessible. No journal quartile was assigned. CCF’s March 2026 catalogue was not retrieved as a usable list, so no CCF rank was applied. The category names in the project table mention CCF because that is the project’s bucket label; `Ranking_System` is CORE only.

OpenAlex was not used. Crossref work types from the existing cache were not re-fetched.
